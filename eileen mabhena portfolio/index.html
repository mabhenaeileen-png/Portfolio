01 · About

I want to use technology to build better healthcare systems for Southern Africa.

I'm a Computer Science and Informatics student at the University of Johannesburg. I'm driven, team-minded, and happiest when I'm learning something new or being challenged by a different perspective. My personal interests are backend development, software engineering, and health.

Watching a family member go without the help they needed because the technology wasn't there is what pointed me toward this field. I believe the link between technology and health is underestimated, and that computer scientists carry a real responsibility to their countries.

As a woman in STEM, I also want to show younger girls that this path is open to them too. Outside of class, I play for the UJ Stars basketball team and tutor my peers.
